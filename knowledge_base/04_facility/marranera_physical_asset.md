## CIRCULATION FLOW — BIOSECURITY

Target flow is unidirectional, avoiding cross-contamination between zones:

1. Raw materials enter **Zona Norte** from the north.
2. Pasteurized substrate crosses to the **Marranera** from the west for inoculation.
3. Finished product exits **Zona Sur** from the south for dispatch.

Three independent access points support this sequence. No structural modifications are currently required to establish the intended directional flow.
